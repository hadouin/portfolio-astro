## Design Context

### Users
Prospective clients, collaborators, and hiring teams evaluating Hadouin Leroy's creative-development work. They need to understand his positioning quickly, assess the quality and range of his work, and find a direct path to contact or hire him.

### Brand Personality
Confident, technically sharp, and editorial. The portfolio should feel personal and authored rather than templated, balancing creative character with professional clarity.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined monochrome with strong black-and-white photography, charcoal neutrals sampled from the imagery, expressive scale, and asymmetric editorial compositions. Avoid generic SaaS styling, decorative gradients, neon-on-dark effects, and excessive card-based layouts.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with one memorable visual idea and keep supporting elements restrained.
2. Use typography, contrast, and spacing to establish hierarchy before adding decoration.
3. Preserve the portfolio's direct path from identity to work to contact.
4. Treat photography as part of the layout, using considered crops and tonal fades rather than boxed images.
5. Keep motion purposeful, accessible, and secondary to the content.
